Structure without the pressure

Focus and break timers, water reminders, and blockers, delivered in a friendlier way.

I noticed I drifted to YouTube during my own study sessions and wanted help focusing and managing my time.
So I designed An ambient companion that floats over your work with timers, reminders and nudges. Postpone a break in one tap (+30s, +1m, +5m); the window tucks away for full screen.
Evaluated via Dogfooding during daily study sessions to stress-test nudge timing, window persistence, and break postponement in real desktop workflows.

Designed in Figma first

Session, reminders, schedule, app rules, progress, buddy screens, break overlays, and the Mambo widget, in light and dark themes on shared tokens (Fredoka, DM Sans).

From frame to working overlay

The break overlay is the heart of the product: a calm card with a small plus to postpone. Figma held a placeholder; the build plays Mambo's states, with a gentler nudge for distracting sites.

Working with AI, on purpose

I directed the AI and kept the product decisions. Each round followed one loop.

I designed Frames, tokens and reference screenshots in Figma. Every round starts by comparing the running app to them.
I directed I wrote each change in plain language; Gemini structured it into a prompt, and Gemini 4 Argon generated the Electron, React and TypeScript.
I reviewed I ran the app, clicked every screen, and wrote the next change list. Saved transcripts show 10 rounds (stages 9 to 18).

Where human review changed the product

  • Cut clutter: the Messages tab and a focus-timer pill
  • Made the window behave like a normal app: resizable, no fullscreen, close tucks into Mambo
  • Fixed low-contrast light-mode text against WCAG AA (formal audit still to do)
  • One purple palette instead of blue and purple, with hover states on every desktop button
  • Confirmation dialogs before delete, clear and sign-out actions
  • Removed settings that did not work yet (cloud sync, "iPhone connected")

The desktop app

Editable reminders, a schedule for study blocks, and app rules that nudge you when a listed app or site opens mid-session. Auto-starting sessions from the schedule is next.

Companions and progress

Mambo is the starting companion; levels unlock more, and each companion's animations can link to a reminder, session or alert. A progress screen tracks focus hours, streaks and level.

What's next

Rolling updates, desktop first.

  • Multi-user feedback loops: gathering longitudinal telemetry on nudge postponement intervals
  • Smart activation: auto-detecting study domains to launch Mambo sessions seamlessly
  • Mobile companion sync: extending session timers across phone and desktop
  • WCAG AA compliance audit: formal contrast verification across dark and light themes
