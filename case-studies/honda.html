Honda of Clear Lake

A usability audit of a real car dealership's website, evaluated against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics to find where real car shoppers were losing confidence.

Role
Solo UX Auditor
Team
Solo project
Scope
Practice audit
Tools
Figma, FigJam
01 — The problem

Shoppers couldn't shop with confidence

Buying a car is already a high-stakes decision. Walking through the live site as a first-time shopper, the friction wasn't a single broken feature — it was a pattern of small heuristic violations (inconsistent navigation, unclear pricing, weak error recovery) that each chipped away at trust before a shopper ever reached financing.

02 — Research → decision → evaluation

From violation to recommendation

We learned Running the site against Nielsen's 10 heuristics surfaced repeated violations of visibility of system status and consistency/standards — shoppers couldn't always tell what filters were active or what a click would do before doing it.
So I changed I wrote prioritized, severity-ranked recommendations (not a redesign) tied to each violation — specific fixes like persistent filter state, clearer inventory and pricing labeling, and consistent navigation patterns across pages.
Evaluated via A severity-rating pass against Nielsen's own 0–4 scale, so the recommendations read as a prioritized punch list a real team could act on, not a flat list of opinions.
03 — Outcome

A prioritized fix list, not just a critique

The deliverable was a heuristic evaluation report: every violation tied to a specific heuristic, rated by severity, and paired with a concrete recommendation — the format a real stakeholder could hand to engineering without translation.
