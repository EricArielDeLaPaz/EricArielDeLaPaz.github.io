Honda of Clear Lake

A usability audit of a real car dealership's website, evaluated against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics to find where real car shoppers were losing confidence.

Heuristic evaluation
Role
Solo UX Auditor

Shoppers couldn't shop confidently

Buying a car is already high-stakes. Walking the live site as a first-time shopper, the friction wasn't one broken feature, it was a pattern of small heuristic violations (inconsistent navigation, unclear pricing, weak error recovery) that each chipped away at trust before a shopper ever reached financing.

I walked the full dealership experience across five areas: Navigation, Homepage, Inventory, Vehicle Details and Checkout. I rated every finding by severity, and Navigation produced the most.

From violation to fix

I learned Running the site against Nielsen's 10 heuristics surfaced repeated violations of visibility of system status and consistency/standards, shoppers couldn't always tell what filters were active or what a click would do before doing it.
So I changed I wrote prioritized, severity-ranked recommendations (not a redesign) tied to each violation, specific fixes like persistent filter state, clearer inventory and pricing labeling, and consistent navigation patterns across pages.
Evaluated via A severity-rating pass against Nielsen's own 0–4 scale, so the recommendations read as a prioritized punch list a real team could act on, not a flat list of opinions.

Three catastrophic findings, three fixes

Three findings were rated catastrophic. Each one pairs the problem with the fix I recommended in the report.

2.5.0 · HomepageCatastrophic

Map controls cut off

The embedded map isn't centered, so some map controls are cut off.

Heuristic: User Control & Freedom, Consistency & Standards

Recommended fixRecenter the map so all controls are fully visible in the default view.
3.0.0 · InventoryCatastrophic

Search bar locks scrolling

Clicking into the search bar makes the page unscrollable, locking the user into the search input until they exit it.

Heuristic: User Control & Freedom

Recommended fixAllow normal page scrolling while search is active, and avoid modal-like behavior unless it is a deliberate full-screen search.
5.3.0 · CheckoutCatastrophic

Accessibility button missing

The accessibility button is not available on the checkout screen, even though it appears on other pages.

Heuristic: Help & Documentation

Recommended fixKeep the accessibility button visible and functional throughout the entire checkout flow.

A fix list, not a critique

The deliverable was a heuristic evaluation report: every violation tied to a specific heuristic, rated by severity, and paired with a concrete recommendation, the format a real stakeholder could hand to engineering without translation.
