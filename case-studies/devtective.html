DevTective

A gamified coding-education concept that treats every bug as a case to solve. It responds to a familiar outcome: 5–15% of people who start a free coding course never finish it.

Momentum, not motivation

Interviews with early-stage coders showed a fixable gap: after a few days away, they didn't know where they'd left off.

Three learners

A career changer, a junior designer, and a marketer: who it's for and where each stalls.

Designing for re-entry

I learned The hardest moment: the second session after a gap, rebuilding context.
So I changed Home screen built around one "pick up where you left off" entry with a short recap, and honest streak states without guilt-driven red warnings.
Evaluated via Low-fi prototype walkthroughs: could a returning user say what to do next on the first screen?

The screens

The rest of the concept in high fidelity: case files, build mode, and rewards.

A small design system

Color tokens and a typographic scale keep every screen consistent.

A concept built to test

Deliverable: a problem statement, a re-entry-first IA, and hi-fi wireframes ready for usability testing.
