DevTective

DevTective is a gamified coding education app and website that helps learners build real HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills by treating every bug as a case to investigate and solve. The concept responds to a familiar outcome: 5–15% of people who start a free coding course never finish it.

Role
Solo UX Designer
Team
Solo project
Scope
Certificate capstone
Tools
Figma, FigJam

Momentum, not motivation

Most dropout analysis treats this as a motivation problem. Interviewing early-stage coders surfaced a more fixable gap: people didn't quit because they stopped caring — they quit after a few days away left them unsure where they'd left off.

Designing for re-entry

We learned The hardest moment wasn't lesson 1 — it was the second session, after a gap, when a learner had to reconstruct context before they could do anything.
So I changed I designed the home screen around a single "pick up where you left off" entry point with a short context recap, instead of a generic course-list home — and wireframed progress states that show streaks honestly, without guilt-driven red/warning styling.
Evaluated via Low-fidelity prototype walkthroughs checking whether a returning user could state what they'd do next within the first screen, with no explanation needed.

A concept built to test

As a certificate capstone, the deliverable is the research-to-wireframe process itself: a documented problem statement, a re-entry-first information architecture, and wireframes ready for usability testing — the same workflow I'm now applying on paid internship work.
