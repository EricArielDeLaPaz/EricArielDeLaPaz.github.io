SecureID — Case Study

Solo UX Designer
UX ResearchInformation ArchitecturePrototypingWireframingDesign Tokens

Problem: One identity-verification flow had to serve anxious end users and time-pressed internal analysts.

Designed the end-to-end verification flow and a separate analyst case-review dashboard on one shared token system.

Outcome: One token system across two interfaces, a three-action review screen, and status colors mapped one-to-one to meaning.

Honda of Clear Lake — Heuristic Evaluation

Solo UX Auditor
UX PrinciplesAccessibilityUsability AnalysisHeuristic EvaluationSeverity Rating

Problem: Shoppers could not confidently find inventory, pricing, or financing on the dealership site.

Ran a 10-point Nielsen heuristic evaluation and wrote severity-ranked, actionable recommendations.

Outcome: Three catastrophic findings, each paired with a specific fix and ranked by severity.
